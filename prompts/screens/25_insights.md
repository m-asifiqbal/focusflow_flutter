## Purpose
Productivity analytics across modules with ranges.

## Layout (top → bottom)
1. Large header 'Insights' + share button.
2. Range chips: 7 Days (default), Today, 30 Days, 90 Days, Custom.
3. 4 KPI cards: Task completion 84% ▲12%, Focus hours 24.5 ▲4h, Habit consistency 72% ▼3%, Notes created 18 ▲5 (green up / rose down).
4. Line chart 'Daily productivity' (smooth curve, gradient fill, white dots with emerald stroke) with weekday labels.
5. Donut 'Where your time went' (Tasks, Habits, Notes, Reminders) + centre total + legend %.
6. Habit streaks list with progress bars.
7. Weekly tip card (emerald gradient, lightbulb).

## Interactions & behaviour
- Charts via fl_chart with touch tooltips.
- Share → render the page to image/PDF.
- Tips generated by local rules (e.g. best time of day).
